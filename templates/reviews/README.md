# Review Templates

Use the single [change-review template](change-review.md) for every review. Complete its functional, non-functional, design, and architecture lenses only to the depth justified by risk.

- Low/moderate changes may use one reviewer and mark irrelevant lenses `Not applicable` with a reason.
- For high/critical changes, identify the specialist or project review process to consult; this record does not replace it.
- Record reviewer identity or agent/tool and the reviewed revision. Do not describe a review as independent without demonstrable reviewer separation.

A finding without evidence and impact is incomplete. The template records technical readiness, not PR approval.
